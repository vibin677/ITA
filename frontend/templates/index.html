💡 Why Tree Ensembles Win on Agricultural Datasets

Crop yield depends strongly on non-linear threshold effects (e.g. fertilizer boosts yield up to an optimal saturation point, after which excess chemical burning reduces yield). Gradient Boosting and Random Forest naturally capture these multi-way interactions and resist outliers, whereas Linear Regression assumes strictly linear relationships.
